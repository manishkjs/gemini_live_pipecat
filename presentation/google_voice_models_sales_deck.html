Difficult Inbound vs. Delight Experience: Architectural Dichotomy

Why Cascades Dominate Compliance Inbound While Realtime S2S Powers Customer Retention

🛡️ Difficult Inbound (Modular Cascade) Compliance & Redaction
Target Environment: 8kHz PSTN narrowband telephony, loud street noise, crying children, heavy traffic.
  • Phrase Biasing: Chirp 2 accepts explicit class phrases (50,000+ rare pharma drugs or banking SKU codes).
  • PII Interception: Application intercepts raw STT text, runs Cloud DLP to redact Aadhaar/PAN before LLM ingestion.
  • Verbatim Compliance: Chirp 3 HD speaks exact statutory legal disclaimers without risk of LLM phrasing drift.
  • Trade-Off: 2.8s cumulative latency & stripped emotional prosody, but 100% auditable.
✨ Delight Experience (Gemini Live S2S) Intimacy & Retention
Target Environment: 16kHz/24kHz wideband (Mobile Apps, WebRTC, Wearables, Smart TVs).
  • Acoustic Prosody: Understands hesitation, sighs, laughter, and tone; responds with genuine human empathy.
  • Sub-500ms Cadence: Eliminates the awkward 3-second silence that causes 45%+ user drop-offs.
  • Thinker-Talker Duplex: Speaks continuous verbal filler while executing asynchronous background CRM calls.
  • Natural Barge-In: User can interject mid-sentence naturally without breaking state.

Section 4 • Field Lessons & Gotchas

Cascaded Pipeline Failure Modes: The 2.85s Latency Waterfall

Why 3 Disjoint APIs Break Conversational Flow, Strip Acoustic Affect, and Multiply Failure Points

💥 1. Error Propagation Cascade Catastrophic Drift
STT misspelling of an Indic noun ("Mera folio 402 hai" $\to$ "polio 402") causes LLM to reason on false premises, outputting hallucinated advice.
😶 2. Prosody & Emotion Stripping Loss of Affect
Converting speech to plain text discards pitch, hesitation, urgency, and distress. TTS speaks back with neutral, robotic delivery.
🔌 3. Multi-Vendor SLA Fragility 3x Outage Risk
3 vendor APIs = 3 points of rate-limit failure and complex client-side state synchronization for barge-in audio cutting.
⏱️ Turnaround Latency Breakdown: Cascade (2.85s) vs Gemini Live (420ms) Awkward Pause
⚠️ The 2.8-Second Wall: Over 45% of users hang up during the unnatural 2.8s silence. Gemini Live responds in 420ms.
