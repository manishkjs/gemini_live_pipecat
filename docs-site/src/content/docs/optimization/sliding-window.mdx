By default, an uncompressed audio session accumulates ~1,840 carried tokens per minute at 6 turns/min (~235 audio tokens of caller speech and played model audio plus ~72 transcript text tokens per turn) and hits the hard uncompressed-audio session ceiling at 15 minutes. Worse, by Minute 6 (Turn 37), every new turn re-reads 11,000+ historical tokens.

Adding `contextWindowCompression` with a `SlidingWindow` to your `LiveConnectConfig` tells the Gemini Live server to truncate the oldest turns automatically once the context crosses your `trigger_tokens` threshold, while always keeping your `system_instruction` intact.

<ContextCompressionFlow />

## Production configuration

```python
from google.genai import types

config = types.LiveConnectConfig(
    response_modalities=[types.Modality.AUDIO],
    context_window_compression=types.ContextWindowCompressionConfig(
        # Start pruning oldest turns once the context reaches 5,000 tokens
        trigger_tokens=5000,
        sliding_window=types.SlidingWindow(
            # Retain the most recent 3,500 tokens (Pinned systemInstruction + active turns)
            target_tokens=3500
        ),
    ),
)
```

## Choosing `trigger_tokens` and `target_tokens`

- High-efficiency telephony default (`5,000 / 3,500`): Set `trigger_tokens=5000` and `target_tokens=3500` to flush stale `$3.00/1M` carried audio (caller + played model speech) early and keep Time-to-First-Audio-Byte fast. Keep your pinned `systemInstruction` and active tool schemas under ~1,500 tokens so they fit inside the 3,500-token target window. Pair this with [Pillar 2 (Dynamic Prompt Cards)](/gemini_live_pipecat/optimization/prompt-cards/) and [Pillar 3 (Lossless Fact Pruning)](/gemini_live_pipecat/optimization/history-pruning/) so the active step card and facts from Minute 1 stay in `systemInstruction` when old history turns are trimmed.
- Extended raw-context window (`16,000 / 10,000`): If your workflow does not maintain a server-side `FactStore` and needs 4 to 5 minutes of verbatim unpruned audio history in the window, increase `trigger_tokens=16000` and `target_tokens=10000`.
