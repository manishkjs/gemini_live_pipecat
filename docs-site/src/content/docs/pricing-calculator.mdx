## 2. Monthly fleet volume comparison

For a typical 3-minute contact center interaction (6 turns per minute, 4,500-token full SOP), unmanaged sessions compound rapidly because historical audio ($3.00/1M tokens) and full system prompts ($0.75/1M tokens) are re-evaluated on every turn without a caching discount.

| Monthly Call Volume | Total Minutes | Indian BPO Human Labor ($0.025/min) | Unmanaged Gemini Live ($0.082/min) | Optimized Gemini Live ($0.031/min) | Monthly Savings (Optimized vs. Unmanaged) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1,000 calls** | 3,000 mins | $75 | $246 | $93 | **$153 / mo** |
| **10,000 calls** | 30,000 mins | $750 | $2,460 | $930 | **$1,530 / mo** |
| **100,000 calls** | 300,000 mins | $7,500 | $24,600 | $9,300 | **$15,300 / mo** |
| **500,000 calls** | 1,500,000 mins | $37,500 | $123,000 | $46,500 | **$76,500 / mo** |

<Aside type="tip" title="Why longer calls widen the gap">
In an unmanaged session, turn $N$ re-bills the audio from turns $1 \dots N-1$. A 10-minute support call at 6 turns per minute executes 60 turns, causing quadratic token accumulation. Capping the context window at 5,000 tokens flattens this curve into a linear cost per minute.
</Aside>

## 3. The three levers that flatten the cost curve

You do not need to sacrifice native duplex latency or fall back to a high-latency three-model cascade to hit your target budget. Three configuration changes bring Gemini Live down to **$0.028–$0.035 per minute**:

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="1. Ephemeral audio window (5k compaction)" icon="setting">
    Configure `contextWindowCompression` with `trigger_tokens: 5000`. Older raw audio turns ($3.00/1M tokens) are automatically evicted once the window fills, preventing quadratic re-billing while preserving the pinned system instruction and recent dialogue.
  </Card>

  <Card title="2. Just-in-Time (JIT) prompt cards" icon="rocket">
    Keep the initial `systemInstruction` under 500 tokens (`&lt;$0.001` connection cost). Inject domain-specific SOP cards and tool schemas via server-side transcript hooks only after caller intent is verified, avoiding upfront token burn on 0–5 second dropped calls.
  </Card>

  <Card title="3. Minimal thinking budget" icon="approve-check">
    Set `thinkingLevel: "minimal"` in `generationConfig.thinkingConfig` on `gemini-3.1-flash-live-preview`. This trims reasoning token overhead ($4.50/1M output tokens) and shaves 150–250 ms off Time-to-First-Audio-Byte (TTFB).
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
